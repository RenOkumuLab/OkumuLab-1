Each slot moves its target by amount × source in the target's own travel: +100 % with the source at 1 pushes the knob across its whole slider. Every pipe has its own velocity, key, envelope, level, overblowing and pitch deviation, so one chord can be shaped differently in each pipe. Amber markers on the side panels show where the matrix holds each knob now.

Try Overblow → Open/Stopped: the moment a pipe overblows, its top closes.

The Temperament knob (above) tunes the organ: equal temperament, a historical temperament built from its circle of fifths, or Custom, the scale loaded here. Pitch a¹ is the frequency a¹ is tuned to.

A tuned key sounds the pipe of the nearest equal-tempered key, its body cut to the tuned length, as an organ builder tunes it: the mouth follows the pitch (Cut-up follow), and Pitch lock holds the pipe on its tuned pitch.

Without a .kbm the scale starts on c¹ and a¹ is tuned to the Pitch knob; a .kbm brings its own reference and may leave keys out (they sound nothing). A .scl or .kbm dropped anywhere on the screen is loaded too. The files are kept in the project.

    Stop menuPrinzipal 8' (round metal, open) or Gedackt 8' (wood, stopped: Open/Stopped draws its stopper out). Every knob goes to the stop as built; RESET returns to that stop's own defaults

Temperament on Custom plays them; the button is lit amber while the organ is not in equal temperament at 440 Hz

    LAB (PLUGIN)
    
        
    
          
    NickingNicks on the languid: a thicker, less amplifying jet; calmer speech, less chiff and noise
    
          
    Side holeAn open hole in the wall, its place along the body. It is a tone hole: it raises the pitch by its acoustic interval (most near the middle of the bore); pitch lock removes only the jet's pull
    
          
    FM targetPhysical FM at audio rate of the wind, the pipe length (bore delay) or the upper lip (labium offset)
    
          
    Cross driveEach jet is also shaken by the other sounding pipes' mouth flow
    
          
    Notes 24–96The compass of both stops, C, (32.7 Hz) to c⁴ (2093 Hz); other notes sound nothing
    
          
    Temperament · Pitch a¹The organ's tuning: equal, Pythagorean, 1/4-comma meantone, Werckmeister III, Kirnberger III, Vallotti, Young, or a loaded Scala scale; a¹ 380–480 Hz. Every pipe is cut to its tuned pitch. Choosing another stop keeps the tuning
    
          
    Ears · Arched cut-upThe Gedackt's mouth: side ears (their air plug lengthens the mouth's end correction: a little flatter, a clearer speech) and a semicircular upper lip (the jet crosses the mouth in strips of different lengths: a rounder tone)
