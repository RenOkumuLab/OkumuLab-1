LFO · ENVELOPE

Each slot moves its target by amount × source in the target's own travel: +100 % with the source at 1 pushes the knob across its whole slider. Every pipe has its own velocity, key, envelope, level, overblowing and pitch deviation, so one chord can be shaped differently in each pipe. Amber markers on the side panels show where the matrix holds each knob now.

Try Overblow → Open/Stopped: the moment a pipe overblows, its top closes.

    SCREEN
    
        
    
          
    Drag the pipeRotate
    
          
    Mouse wheelZoom (double-click to reset)
    
          
    SlidersDrag or scroll. Shift-drag for fine steps, double-click for the default
    
          
    RESETEvery parameter back to its default (OUT level and view settings stay)
    
          
    Right-click a sliderMIDI learn: move a controller to assign it (right-click again to cancel)
    
          
    Recipe menuExperiment recipes (the host's programs): the canonical pipe with a few knobs moved; the top bar says what to listen for
    
          
    MATRIXModulation matrix (LAB panel): four slots, source → target × amount, per pipe. Amber markers show where it holds each knob
    
          
    WIND / MALLETBlow the pipe, or strike its body with a hard mallet. Struck: velocity is the mallet speed, key up brings the felt damper (pedal holds it off); dimmed sliders do nothing to a struck pipe

    LAB (PLUGIN)
    
        
    
          
    NickingNicks on the languid: a thicker, less amplifying jet; calmer speech, less chiff and noise
    
          
    Side holeAn open hole in the wall, its place along the body. It is a tone hole: it raises the pitch by its acoustic interval (most near the middle of the bore); pitch lock removes only the jet's pull
    
          
    FM targetPhysical FM at audio rate of the wind, the pipe length (bore delay) or the upper lip (labium offset)
    
          
    Cross driveEach jet is also shaken by the other sounding pipes' mouth flow
    
          
    Notes 0–127Pipes beyond the compass: the scale is extrapolated (20 m pipes at the bottom, a few mm at the top)
